Green Bay Packers @ Minnesota Vikings Line — Packers +1.5

Let's start the season with an intra-division battle!! The Packers visit Minnesota to take on the Vikings in the CBS feature game of the week.

The Packers always pretty good have been mostly quiet this pre-season as they wait for the return of Micah Parsons and Josh Jacobs. The Vikings had a decent 2025 season despite poor QB play and hope the addition of Kyler Murray will return them to their 2024 form.

Looking forward to this early test for the Vikings — GB in Week 1 then the Bears in Week 2. Vikings have a chance to shake things up early in the NFC North race.

New Orleans Saints @ Detroit Lions Line — Lions -7.5

The last time the Lions played the Saints in the season opener it was Matthew Stafford's pro debut in 2009.

On defense for the Lions, hopes are for a more consistent pass rush with new additions including second round pick Derrick Moore. Will also be interesting to see how the the secondary holds up missing it's two starters at safety (Kerby Joseph and Brian Branch) and former first round pick Terrion Arnold.

On offense, this will be the second year in a row with a new coordinator. Drew Pitzing promises a return to a more balanced attack. An upgraded offensive line includes first round pick Blake Miller.

Chicago Bears vs Carolina Panthers Line — Bears -3

Two of the more surprising (and lucky) teams of 2025 face off in the season's first game. For Chicago, it's how much better can their QB be in season three and if the defense can find a new identity after losing a few pieces from last year.
